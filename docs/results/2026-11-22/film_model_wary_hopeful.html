Two maps

Two creatures explore the same hidden ground, each in its own copy, and learn it only through what their bodies feel. The middle map is what is really there. On each side is what one creature believes, drawn with the same colours, so wherever belief and world differ, the colours differ. Point at a tile, tap it, or tab into a map and use the arrow keys, to read its numbers.

The key to the colours

For each tile a creature holds five beliefs from 0 to 1 (it blocks me, it hurts, it feeds, it quenches, it pleases) and counts its tries. Three lights fill the tile: red as bright as hurts, green as bright as feeds, blue as bright as quenches; they add, so feeding and hurting together is yellow. A warm glow is pleases. A stone slab is something that blocks and does nothing else.

When it is not sure

Each light is as bright as the belief. Hatched mist lies over a tile that has not been tried, and thins with every try.
