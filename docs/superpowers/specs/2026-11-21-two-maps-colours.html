Belief as colour

Reference for the drawing rule of the two-maps design. Every number is invented. One drawing rule for all three maps: the middle one is the world as it is, the outer two are what each creature believes. Point at a tile to read its numbers.

What a creature holds for each tile

Five beliefs from 0 to 1, and one count: it blocks me · it hurts · it feeds · it quenches · it pleases · times tried.

When it is sure

Three lights fill the tile: red for hurts, green for feeds, blue for quenches. A warm glow is pleases. A stone slab is something that blocks and does nothing else.

When it is not sure

Each light is as bright as the belief. Hatched mist lies over a tile that has not been tried, and thins with every try.
